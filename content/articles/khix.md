## starter paragraph

hello ladies and gentleladies, i am back, KHIX is over. wow, that was alot. im writing this on 11/11/26 the one month of KHIX being over and im still processing everything. first of all, i want to thank the entire team again. from hackorg who helped put it on and run both hacks this year, to the club teams, workshop, outreach, sponsorship, design, and dev who helped make the year easier and also contributed to posting and the hack. some legends on these teams who without we couldnt have done it. so thank yall if you read this.

## the build up

honestly, it was relatively chill until the end, we were pretty smooth sailing, not too much i can really say to the public... some issues but it really started hitting about a month and a half out. I think from the period of August to until the hack ended, all I did everyday was Knight Hacks. eat sleep breathe knight hacks, for a big majority of time i was the only exec onsite, which made things a little tricky especially with handling issues. with that, and my duties as treasurer mainly being all in person stuff, like negotiating with everyone, it was honestly a bit tiring. especially for me im pretty introverted and i kinda have to bring it out of me sometimes to public speak like that all the time. for awhile though, it was looking a little grim, thats life tho, with stuff you pour your life into, it always looks grim at some point.

## the merch debacle

this was probably our weakest point of the entire event, i wont get into details because i cant, but lets just say the merch was in my apartment on wednesday 10/7. as you may know, the hack started 10/9. so that means everything had to be bundled in 2 days, we did it in a few hours, luckily having such a big team makes it easy to do stuff like that on the fly, like 10 people showed up in a few hours notice and bang we went crazy.

the lanyards and badges wouldnt show until 10/8, and we missed out on posters. which sucked but me and my roommates just solo'd the lanyards, also some of the team randomly showed up it was like the day before and they.... contributed (arthur LOL).

adrian and his family also just solo'd the entire packaging of the lennys. which was real convenient since i was running out of space in my apartment... speaking of

## my apartment

oh boy oh boy where do i begin... THAT WAS THE WORST THING OF ALL TIME. so bad that knight hacks now owns a 10x10 storage unit, my room had 2 walkways about the width of me to my bed, and to my bathroom, all other negative space was completely packed to the brim with prizes, merch, you name it i owned it. now that i think about it i probably had atleast 25k worth of stuff just sitting in my apartment. towards the end of it the living room was even packed to the brim. you gotta do what you gotta do, but we didnt have to do that lol. its ok tho id rather have the prizes safe...

## day of

i think my performance on the first day was the worst of all the days, honestly, i dont really get nervous but this was one of the days i was for sure, the plan was i was going to sleep until 2pm while adrian covers since i was going to stay up until adrian got back from his sleep, so it would be like 9am-11pm for adrian then 3pm- saturday 8am for me. fortunately or unfortunately for me, that plan was thrown out the window by like 9:30am friday. I went to go drop off the keys to my apartment to my cousin who was in town that weekend for the hack, and turns out the team left the walkies in the apartment, i was at jeffs bagels minding my own business, waiting for my cousin eating my bagel and i get a call from dylan, guys panting and hes like we... need the... walkies we keep having to run around, that was so funny i was dying on the other side of the phone, eating my bagels, calm before the storm tai shee. anyway go back to the apartment, pick up the walkies, dylans calling me screaming about where are u where are u. something i think im good at is being calm, and patient, i think in situations like that, all i can do is chuckle cause everyone knows that i cant teleport, i have to drive, traffic lights, over. dylan however, maybe from exhaustion didnt find it so funny, i did tho...

anywho i get there and i end up waking up at 9am and going to sleep at 9am saturday, while also sleeping 4 hours on thursday night, which for me, is not great. i like my sleep and ontop of that organizing a hack is constant running around and thinking and speaking so its pretty tiring. was okay though once everything was setup and check in started, i deadass stopped in my tracks and sat there for a second, looking at the line and how smooth everything was going, smiles and peacefulness, thats when i started taking it in. holy fuck were doing it. it was so good, checkin was beautiful, and i was father fungus, getting to talk to everyone and seeing everyone so full energy and happy, all the sponsors arriving and meeting most of them in person for the first time, it was such a feeling ill never forget.

### opening

opening was chill, honestly, if i took anything away from khix its that next time im speaking, im prepping some more beforehand mane, idk if i blanked but i def felt for opening that i was not talking well. i think recently ive kinda been on a bad talking spree but i think its cause im doing it too much. or maybe im raising my standards. the most notable thing was that my hat fell off twice in a row, which it was kinda funny the first time so good bit and then it fell off again and my hat hair did not help. i was wearing that hat for like 5 hours already, man.... but other then that it was smooth. honestly i always skip out on opening ceremonies for hacks because i think their too long, and yes, i still think that

### sponsor fair

i cant talk about the 2 little mishaps we had, but it was chill, actually scrap that it was hot as hell, i still have no idea why the ac wasnt working like usual, its okay though i made a little decision to get people water and i think that helped enough our circumstance, that thing was packed though idk career fairs at hacks i feel will always be bad, its fundamentally not a scalable event (BUT WE FIXED THAT).

### rest of friday

my family, mom, cousin and crew pulled up so that was fire, they ran the michelin star popup which was great and it was good finally seeing them and showing them what ive been up too, its kinda full circle moment for me because before this i was in esports, and its really hard to show like look theres all these people, but now in this, theres all these people and they are right infront of you so that was amazing.

### saturday

i went to sleep saturday at like 8am and ngl i woke up at like 11, i have trouble sleeping during hacks already but our hack? yea gg, carlos landed tho so getting to see him on saturday it was nice and i think saturday in general was my favorite day, alot of dilly dallying and just like enjoying the hack rather then being in the mines for everyone,

### scav hunt and misc activities

first came my cousins second popup, which went great and the lemon olive oil cake he made was actually really good, the rave and all that happened and my other cousin, who goes here and her roommates pulled up. i dont think they were expecting to see me as father fungus, but that is how that goes sometimes. they all stayed for the scav hunt, which was fucking hilarious seeing them just ultra confused, my cousin leo (the chef) also participated where i gave him a lost hacker saying he had a clue and leo just bolted away.

### note on my family

i think my favorite part about my family being there is the absolute confusion they all had, i like to purposely not explain things fully so that people form their own opinions so i really did not tell them much going into it, as a result my mom, i felt kinda bad, was really concerned about my ways of working. she was getting me food and stuff which i was super appreciative of but you know, thats just how this goes sometimes you just got to work and work and no way im letting people down at this point. My other cousin too sara, when she brought her roommates a laughed alittle cause thats perfect, i already knew they were gonna be confused as hell. you walk into this engineering event and theres a rave going on, everyones dressed up, this scavenger hunt starts and theres this whole stupid scene going on, perfection.

## da end

i dont wanna make this one too long, but by sunday i was lowkey feeling like butt, we were trying to close out and all in all, everything went smooth, judging done in 4 hours flat, closing getting everyone there was a slight mishap, some crowd control needed to be done, i think the pressure was getting to everyone but overall it was pretty smooth.

I definitely felt more comfortable speaking at closing rather then opening, i gave a little spheal about family and hacking and all that, we had some good stories win. it was great, thank you to all the sponsors, food companies we worked with, merch, volunteers, mentors we couldnt have asked for a better event

LONG LIVE KNIGHT HACKS!!
